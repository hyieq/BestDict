https://github.com/argosopentech/argospm-index
https://www.argosopentech.com/
Original OPUS-MT model authors: Jörg Tiedemann and Santhosh Thottingal.
"OPUS-MT — Building open translation services for the World", EAMT 2020,
Lisbon, Portugal.
Original OPUS models are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Upstream attribution files are preserved in the user library under
argos/models/en_zh/README.md and argos/models/zh_en/README.md.
